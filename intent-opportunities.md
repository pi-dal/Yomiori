## Shared identity, access and system-surface policy

List identity is the **case-sensitive `WordListDocument.id`**; entry identity is the tuple **`(document.id, entry.id)`** (C3–C4, C6). UTF-8 IDs are hex-encoded for filenames to preserve case distinctions on case-insensitive filesystems. Titles, terms, filenames, source URLs and attribution text are display/search metadata, not identity. Same term in different lists or homographs must remain distinct. `ielts` is reserved for the bundled list and cannot be imported. Existing list language and translation language cannot change. Source metadata is supplied by the document; validation does not authenticate its author, rights or trustworthiness.

Proposed entity/query resolution must await library loading, surface load errors, resolve exact IDs again at execution and return a missing/stale result rather than substituting a similar title or word. Human text matches require disambiguation using list, language and entry context. Metadata/content can change while IDs persist. Imported IDs survive relaunch through catalogue files but can disappear after clear-data. Saved Cards have separate UUIDs and remain snapshots when catalogue entries change; word-list IDs must not be treated as material/PDF `sourceID` values.

No app account/login/owner authorization appears in the inspected local chains. That does not prove device-authentication or lock-screen behavior. A future query and execution path must both stay within this installation's local data; protected-data access outside an active view is untested. Imported content, reading titles, notes, sentences and logs may be private. Do not expose broad private content through indexing or spoken results without a product decision. A file-access grant, app access, device authentication and confirmation of a mutation are separate concerns.

For **each pending action**, the ordinary proposal is a Shortcuts action; its declaration, parameter/result support and OS coverage remain unverified (A1–A5 below). **App Shortcut/Siri phrase exposure** requires separately verified provider/phrase work (A6); it is not inferred from an ordinary action. **Spotlight** discovery/indexing and privacy behavior remain separate, unverified work (A7), with private content indexing deferred. **Apple Intelligence** semantic/schema fit, dependencies and availability are not determined (A8); no schema/domain/member, hardware, language/region or execution support is promised. Source declarations, compilation, visibility, simulator execution and signed-device execution are distinct evidence levels; none of the proposed integrations has reached any of them.
